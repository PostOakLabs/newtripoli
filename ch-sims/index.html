Cognitive Husbandry · interactive companion

The sim catalogue eight billion people wake up to.

After the Intake, every resident of New Tripoli wakes inside a deep catalogue of sims — worlds to live in, at whatever speed they choose. This is that catalogue, made browsable, paired with instruments that let you operate the real ideas underneath: how much life a dilation rate actually buys you, why the Universal Sabbath matters, what the world would cost to build.
