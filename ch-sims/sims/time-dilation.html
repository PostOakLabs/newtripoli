What is the Universal Sabbath?

Roughly every six months of real time, the Human Server Stadium performs scheduled maintenance — hardware checks, cooling system service, reorientation of substrate units to reflect relationship changes. During this window, all residents are automatically "hearthed" back to their home and every speed tier is returned to 1× simultaneously. No one can move outside their home for the duration (~1 hour real time).

This is the only moment in New Tripoli when everyone shares the same present. A resident running at 50,000× and a resident running at 1× are, for those few hours, at the same instant. Live video calls become possible across all speed gaps. Friends and family who run at wildly different rates can have a real conversation — not letters, but a conversation.

Characters describe the approach of a Sabbath window the way pre-Intake cultures described major holidays: as something to prepare for, compose yourself toward, write the letter you've been putting off. For residents at extreme dilation rates, it arrives very rarely by subjective measure — a 1,000× resident experiences 500 subjective years between windows. The Sabbath is called "universal" not as a metaphor but as a literal fact: it is, briefly, universal.

The formula

New Tripoli dilation is a pure clock-rate multiplier. At rate R×, you experience R subjective seconds for every one real (Earth-Prime / HSS 1×) second:

subjective time = R × real time

So per real year you live R subjective years; per six-month maintenance interval you live R × 0.5 subjective years. New Centauri's million-fold rate is exactly the canon line "a million subjective years per calendar year."

Why the Sabbath number matters

At maintenance (§9), every resident is briefly returned to 1× simultaneously — the only moment all speed tiers share a present. The faster you run, the longer the subjective wait between those moments. A 1,000× resident lives ~500 subjective years between two 6-month windows; this is the canon's inverse loneliness problem — synchrony with slower friends becomes rare and precious. It is also why the max coherent sim-story length equals this same figure: a sim can't tell a story longer than the block before everyone is hearthed home. New Tripoli's 50× ceiling over 6 months gives the canon's 25-simulated-year cap.

The contact-list panel (speculative)

The "people met" figure is illustrative — it multiplies your chosen meeting rate by the subjective years you live in one real year. The caps are real estimates: Dunbar's number (~150 stable relationships), the Bernard–Killworth network estimate (~230–290), and a rough lifetime-acquaintance cap (~600). Even modest dilation blows past all three, which is the §30 premise: AI contact-triage exists because no human social faculty was built for this.

Apes run slower (§13)

Great apes are capped at 2× (a few robust chimps reach 4×) against the human 50× ceiling. The "Ape ceiling · 2×" preset shows why Ham and Enos are gradually left behind: at 2× they live one subjective year per six real months, while their human friends at 50× live twenty-five.
