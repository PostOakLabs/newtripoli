Citation & documented record
Documented record
Critical Path Method (CPM), developed late 1950s for industrial project scheduling — the scheduling technique this graph runs. Grid sequence (mine/smelt → wire-draw → wind coils → magnets + prime mover → generator → transmission → load) and the "hidden prerequisite" framing (insulation, lubricants, precision tooling gate the visible steps) follow Lewis Dartnell's civilization-rebuild survey.
Caveat
Task durations are order-of-magnitude illustrative ranges, not a sourced construction schedule — treat every number as a band, never a point. ~90% of real dependency edges in rebuild-style projects are finish-to-start (this graph is built that way throughout).
Genre / further reading (published, not scholarship)
Lewis Dartnell, The Knowledge: How to Rebuild Our World from Scratch (2014) — the flagship popular-science treatment of this exact bootstrap problem.
