Documented record

  • Platt, Autumn in the Heavenly Kingdom (2012) — contingency thesis: "all signs pointed to a Taiping victory" before Western intervention flipped.
  • Spence, God's Chinese Son (1996).
  • Meyer-Fong, What Remains (2013).
  • LSE Working Paper 320 — likin / indirect taxation as fiscal workaround.
Caveat: Ferguson's Virtual History and Cowley's What If? contain no Taiping essay — cited here as Western counterfactual-methodology precedent only, not as a source on this scenario. Census figures for 1850–1864 are contested; treat every number on this page as a range, never a point.

Genre & further reading — incl. the Chinese-language counterfactual tradition

  • 陈舜臣 (Chin Shunshin), 《太平天国》 literary — major historical novel, fictional merchant 连理文 POV; the serious literary anchor.
  • 黄世仲, 《洪秀全演义》 (1908) literary — early novel openly sympathetic to the Taiping; a period pro-Taiping counter-voice to Qing-victor history.
  • 知乎 essay 「架空历史世界观：假如太平天国推翻了清朝」 analytic — argues a Taiping victory fragments China Soviet-civil-war-style; used below as the pessimistic branch.
  • 《太平血》, 《这个天国不太平》, 《乱清》, 《回到太平天国当康王》 web-novel — living participatory genre, labelled color, not scholarship.
